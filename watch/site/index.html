How reporting works

An account is needed to report. Your identity stays private.

  1. Create an account

    Register with your email and verify it.

  2. Add evidence

    Upload photos, choose the location and describe what you saw.

  3. Moderators review

    Nothing goes public until a moderator approves it.

  4. Follow progress

    Track status changes in your account and see verified updates.

Your privacy

Public pages never show who submitted a report.

  • No name, email, phone number or profile photo is shown publicly.
  • Photos are stripped of location data before they are shown.
  • Exact addresses stay private and visible only to authorised reviewers.
  • No public profiles and no follower counts.

Anonymity is not absolute. We keep limited account and security records and may disclose them only where the law requires.

What each status means

We keep allegations, checks and official outcomes clearly separate.

  • Citizen-submitted allegationWhat a person reported. Not yet verified.
  • Checked by moderatorsReviewed for safety and clarity. This does not prove the claim.
  • Referred to an authoritySent to the relevant agency, such as NAFDAC for product safety.
  • Officially confirmedShown only when an authority’s finding is on record.
  • Reported as resolvedRequires a recorded basis or supporting evidence.
